# Method Design — Candidate Principles

This packet presents Candidate Principles for Human discussion and selection. It does not contain a test plan, execution set, or cost approval.

**Solution-space constraint.** UNDERCONSTRAINED: The accepted first RMC and the R6 return jointly constrain the scientific center to an adaptive decision-sufficient contact operator with state-dependent valid margins. ## P-CAPACITY-CONDITIONED-FEASIBLE-INCUMBENT · version 3

**Principle.** Represent continuous soft contact by a minimal calibrated action-conditional capacity state, and use it inside an anytime robust horizon program whose verified feasible incumbent is the only action that may be rendered at any deadline.

**Mechanism.** Encode safe history; predict candidate-impedance-conditioned future margins; calibrate them only on the predeclared support population; use a pre-outcome history-and-action support relation to replace unsupported predictions by the envelope-conservative capacity set; form one robust horizon problem over that exact capacity relation and coupled normal-tangential stiffness/damping; shift or construct and verify an initial feasible policy; commit only fully verified improvements; render the incumbent on timeout, failure or deadline. This changes Environment modeling and optimal impedance become one capacity-to-verified-incumbent applied-policy relation.. Decision-equivalent histories determine the exact robust feasible set, while incumbent persistence makes intended and applied feasible policies identical under interruption.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** Decision-calibrated contact capacity and feasible-incumbent persistence are synthesized into one applied-policy relation.

**Primary risks.** Safe history contains future-margin information that changes impedance decisions. → Narrow the claim or add sensing.; On the declared episode population, supported history-action pairs admit useful one-sided capacity calibration, while the pre-outcome support rule sends every unsupported in-envelope pair to a nonempty envelope-conservative admissible set. → The calibration boundary or robustness claim fails, or the declared operating envelope must shrink.; A robustly feasible incumbent can be initialized and verified for every declared update context. → Deadline totality fails.; Capacity construction and full incumbent verification fit the update budget. → The method is not online executable.

**Substantive difference.** Unlike the certificate candidate, totality depends on persistence and verification of a horizon-policy incumbent; usefulness can survive without a compact controlled-invariant certificate but requires feasible warm starts.

**Killer-test concepts.** Incremental held-out impedance regret and margin prediction from nested histories: Pattern A=Retained history changes gain ranking or outcome, and a smaller state matches the full state within tolerance. vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-CALIBRATED-MEMORYLESS-MODEL-SUFFICIENT Pattern B=Richer history adds no reproducible decision value. under Held-out aliases occur.; Matched history-conditioned versus memoryless capacity relations on the same supported episode-action population: one-sided optimizer-margin coverage, set conservatism, nonempty feasible-action rate and induced robust impedance outcome: Pattern A=The history-conditioned relation meets the predeclared coverage target and, relative to the memoryless relation constrained to that same target, exceeds the predeclared non-equivalence threshold in sharpness, feasible-action retention or induced robust outcome; unsupported pairs are separately caught before outcomes and use the internal envelope-conservative set. vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-CALIBRATED-MEMORYLESS-MODEL-SUFFICIENT Pattern B=The memoryless relation also meets the same coverage target and is equivalent to the history-conditioned relation within the predeclared tolerances for conservatism, feasible-action retention and induced robust outcome. under Capacity-affecting material, geometry, sliding or contact-history aliases occur inside the declared population.; Applied-policy identity, robust feasibility and latency under forced interruption: Pattern A=Every deadline returns the last verified incumbent; extra compute improves cost only. vs PRINCIPLE P-CAPACITY-CONDITIONED-VIABILITY-RELATION Pattern B=Incumbent initialization or verification fails where a certificate backup remains directly evaluable. under Forced interruption near active margins.

## P-CAPACITY-CONDITIONED-VIABILITY-RELATION · version 3

**Mechanism.** Learn the same action-conditional future-margin state; calibrate it only on the predeclared support population; use a pre-outcome history-and-action support relation to replace unsupported predictions by the envelope-conservative capacity set; augment controller state with contact, interaction-energy and previous-gain variables; define a capacity-conditioned controlled-invariant or barrier-certified impedance set from that exact capacity relation; optimize joint performance only within this set; if optimization is incomplete, render the certified backup action that is already a member of the same decision relation. This changes A nominal controller plus external safety override becomes one capacity-conditioned certified admissible-action relation.. Decision-equivalent histories determine the certificate parameters, while the nonempty certified action set makes the selected/backup action feasible and deadline-total without relying on optimizer incumbent persistence.

## P-ADAPTIVE-CONTACT-FEASIBLE-INCUMBENT · version 1

**Principle.** Represent continuous soft contact by a recursively adapted, decision-sufficient action-conditional operator. At every update, classify the pre-outcome context-action pair as supported, unsupported-but-inside a declared physical envelope, or outside that envelope; constrain both informative and performance actions by the margin relation valid for that state; and map the resulting posterior and margins to one total constrained normal-tangential impedance action. Totality is realized by verified-incumbent persistence: an iterate may replace the shifted incumbent only after verification under the same current-state margin relation; interruption returns the incumbent.

**Mechanism.** Construct and adapt the support-aware predictive contact operator, solve the posterior-aware constrained impedance problem by warm-started RTI-SQP, verify candidate sequences against the authoritative M_cal or M_env relation, and atomically replace the shifted feasible incumbent only after verification; timeout or solver failure returns the incumbent. This changes NNBO fixed scalar preidentified contact law -> history- and action-conditioned predictive contact operator with explicit support state -> active in-envelope adaptation under a valid envelope relation -> posterior-aware constrained normal-tangential impedance selection -> one of two explicit totality mechanisms.. The contact operator preserves exactly the history information that changes feasible impedance or its ranking. Calibration-out cases remain operational inside a declared physical envelope: they use an independently justified envelope-conservative margin relation while recursive updates and admissible information actions adapt the posterior. Re-entry to locally calibrated operation requires a predeclared delayed residual audit; it is not inferred from posterior covariance. The second RMC remains open between incumbent persistence and viability certification.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** A support-state-aware adaptive decision-sufficient contact operator that converts calibration-out soft-contact changes into constrained impedance decision recovery rather than detection or refusal, paired with one of two still-competing totality mechanisms. Variant A adds interruption-safe verified-incumbent persistence without making the optimizer itself the paper headline.

**Primary risks.** A compact action-observation history state predicts every future outcome and margin needed to preserve impedance feasibility and ranking over the declared action class. → The representation must expand or the claim envelope must narrow; otherwise contact histories remain decision-aliased.; For every unsupported-but-in-envelope state, declared physics, actuator bounds and contact-retention limits define a nonempty conservative margin relation independently of the adaptive posterior. → The method cannot continue adaptation safely in that state and must narrow the envelope or stop.; In-envelope changes are locally learnable from natural or margin-feasible information actions before their decision relevance expires. → The method can remain conservative but cannot claim online recovery of useful optimal impedance.; After adaptation, a delayed, non-overlapping residual buffer can satisfy the declared local-stationarity and dependence conditions needed for the predeclared empirical calibration audit. → Posterior decision recovery may be observed, but calibrated-margin re-entry cannot be claimed.; A current verified incumbent exists and its shifted sequence remains admissible or can be conservatively repaired before the deadline as the contact state and margin authority change. → The incumbent branch loses totality even if the adaptive contact operator is valid.

**Substantive difference.** Relative to NNBO, the modeled object, update timing and optimizer all change: fixed scalar material parameters become a history/action posterior over joint outcomes and margins; pre-identification becomes continual local adaptation with explicit margin-authority states; and unconstrained scalar optimal control becomes constrained belief-space selection of normal/tangential stiffness and damping. Incumbent and viability mechanisms remain subordinate but scientifically distinct realizations of the accepted totality requirement. Its totality claim depends on persistence of a previously verified sequence, not construction of an invariant set.

**Killer-test concepts.** Matched-compute held-out feasible-set agreement and impedance regret for scalar NNBO state, full physical features and the predictive state: Pattern A=The predictive state matches the high-information teacher within tolerance while scalar NNBO aliases histories and full reconstruction adds no decision value. vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-CALIBRATED-MEMORYLESS-MODEL-SUFFICIENT Pattern B=A calibrated memoryless or scalar contact state is decision-equivalent on the same shifts, margins and action class. under Held-out material, geometry, sliding or loading-history aliases occur.; After an in-envelope shift, posterior prediction error, feasible-action set and constrained impedance regret under passive update versus admissible decision-gated information actions: Pattern A=Both methods remain inside M_env, but recursive adaptation changes the posterior and feasible gains; admissible information actions shorten recovery of decision quality relative to passive adaptation and fixed conservative gains. vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-CONSERVATIVE-FIXED-GAIN-SAFETY Pattern B=The posterior changes without recovering decision quality, or recovery requires inadmissible excitation, so the fixed conservative action is decision-equivalent or superior. under The shift changes active margins and at least one M_env-admissible informative action exists.; State-labelled violations and sharpness under M_cal before shift, M_env during adaptation, and local M_cal after declared re-entry: Pattern A=Every adapting action is covered by the independently valid envelope relation; calibrated re-entry occurs only after the delayed audit meets its target, and then restores useful sharpness. vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-CALIBRATED-MEMORYLESS-MODEL-SUFFICIENT Pattern B=A calibrated memoryless relation meets the same validity and sharpness targets without support-state switching, or the proposed relation re-enters without the declared audit and gains no defensible margin advantage. under A supported pair becomes unsupported but stays in the physical envelope.; Membership of every published gain in the active margin relation under forced interruption, infeasibility and support-state switching: Pattern A=Every deadline returns a previously verified or newly verified incumbent; reduced compute changes cost but not action existence or membership. vs PRINCIPLE P-ADAPTIVE-CONTACT-VIABILITY-RELATION Pattern B=Incumbent shift loses feasibility under fast capacity motion, whereas an invariant viability set still supplies an admissible backup. under A margin becomes active or support authority switches near a deadline.

**Status.** ACTIVE: Active R7 integrated Candidate A. It preserves the re-anchored adaptive contact core and incorporates the reviewed R5 incumbent mechanism as the causal repair for update-time totality.
