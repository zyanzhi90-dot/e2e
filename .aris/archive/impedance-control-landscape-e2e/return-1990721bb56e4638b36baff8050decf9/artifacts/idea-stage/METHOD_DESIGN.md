# Method Design — Candidate Principles

Design cycle: `MDCYCLE-SOFTSCAN-V2-R12-PHYSICAL-CAPACITY-TOTAL-IMPEDANCE`

This packet presents Candidate Principles for Human discussion and selection. It does not contain a test plan, execution set, or cost approval.

**Solution-space constraint.** CONSTRAINED: The accepted envelope is bounded rather than arbitrary, and current direct contact Evidence supports a compact hereditary finite-area physical family while not establishing a decision-relevant residual that survives its bounded online adaptation. A pure learned predictor adds calibration-data and extrapolation assumptions; a physical-plus-learned residual adds both model classes without an independently established residual MUST. Under the strongest-simple-rival rule, the bounded physical decision-outcome route is therefore the unique retained Method. Learned and hybrid representations remain falsifying comparators: physical-family noncontainment or persistent action-changing residual would return this choice to method_design.

## P-HEREDITARY-CONTACT-CAPACITY-TOTAL-IMPEDANCE · version 2

**Principle.** A bounded hereditary finite-area normal-tangential contact-capacity set is updated from the scan history and consumed by one robust, energy-aware, deadline-total four-gain impedance relation.

**Mechanism.** Represent contact by nonnegative generalized-Maxwell normal memory and a bounded elasto-plastic tangential bristle/finite-area limit-surface state; maintain a drift-propagated fixed-facet parameter polytope from force/torque and kinematic consistency; propagate candidate positive normal/tangential stiffness-damping sequences through that set; and atomically apply only a completely verified robust scan member or the preverified unloading member at the update deadline. This changes NNBO's trial-fixed scalar point-contact law and proposal-then-realization chain become a history-bearing set-valued contact-capacity relation whose same member defines the optimized, energy-authorized, actuator-feasible and applied impedance action.. Normal relaxation, area evolution and tangential pre-slip/moment capacity are preserved exactly where they alter robust gain feasibility or ranking. Drift/reset set membership changes the predictive relation after an admitted calibration-out contact without treating confidence as authority. Robust multistep selection and a registered withdrawal make physical, energy, actuator and compute outcomes part of the applied-action relation rather than downstream corrections.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** A set-valued hereditary finite-area contact-capacity model defined by downstream four-gain decisions and embedded in one energy- and deadline-total applied-action relation; not a generic richer contact model and not a neural replacement of HC.

**Primary risks.** A low-order nonnegative generalized-Maxwell normal model plus elasto-plastic pre-slip and finite-area wrench capacity contains the admitted material, geometry, sliding and history effects at the controller-consumed outcome level. → Return to method_design and admit a structured learned residual or learned outcome Candidate; the physical-only Principle is rejected.; Force/torque and kinematic scan history provides consistency strips that contract decision-relevant parameter directions before their action consequence expires. → Online adaptation reduces to conservative refusal and fails the accepted calibration-out performance problem.; Positive diagonal contact-frame gains (k_n,d_n,k_t,d_t) span the action differences required by force regulation, tangential tracking, slip and contact retention in the accepted scan task. → Expand the action class only after matched-budget action-equivalence falsifies the four-gain class.; The discrete interaction-energy balance upper-bounds gain-change, moving-reference, delay and inner-loop mismatch work over one update interval. → The optimizer cannot claim energy-authorized applied gains.; A rate-, actuator- and energy-feasible tangential-stop/normal-unload action exists throughout the declared withdrawal envelope, and its verification/publication WCET fits the reserved deadline. → The total-action relation and RMC2 closure fail; viability or a narrower envelope must be reconsidered.

**Substantive difference.** Relative to NNBO it changes scalar trial-fixed HC into online hereditary finite-area normal-tangential capacity and changes unconstrained one-axis optimal impedance into robust four-gain total action. Relative to deformable-contact MPC it selects impedance and adapts properties in contact. Relative to learned and adaptive MPVIC it makes bounded history/capacity uncertainty, not a point or mean model, authorize the applied scan action.
