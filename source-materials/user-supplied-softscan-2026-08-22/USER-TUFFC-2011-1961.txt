          The indenter is modeled as an analytical surface and
      the sample is partitioned and meshed with the following
      strategy. To reduce the computation time and ensure the
      accuracy of the simulations, a 2 × 2 mm square region (re-
      gion 1 in Fig. 3) is partitioned from the sample part and
      it is meshed uniformly by elements smaller than the rest
      of the sample (region 2 in Fig. 3). The rest of the sample
      is meshed so that the size of the elements gradually in-
      creases moving away from the indentation region. At the
      edges where the two regions touch, the sizes of the mesh-
      ing elements match. Both regions of the sample part are
      meshed with 3-node linear axisymmetric triangles, CAX3
      in Abaqus. For each simulation, a coarse mesh is used first
      and then a finer mesh is tested. This procedure is repeated
      until the simulation result converges.
