# Method Design — Candidate Principles

Design cycle: `MDCYCLE-SOFTSCAN-V2-R15-TRO-MEAN-DEVIATION`

This packet presents Candidate Principles for Human discussion and selection. It does not contain a test plan, execution set, or cost approval.

**Solution-space constraint.** CONSTRAINED: Under the same ultrasound task, gain-only A2-I and nominal-only A2-II have zero gain authority at zero tracking error. Probe-contact NNBO retains an optimal force relation, but force-to-impedance realization is nonunique and singular at zero error; direct four-gain HJB loses authority in the same condition and becomes a state-constrained minimizer, while two-stiffness state-derived damping is nonaffine. Meeting wrench, slip, energy, actuator, rate and deadline limits therefore requires the same constrained predictive relation already present in the T-RO backbone. This causal and feasibility constraint, rather than the solver identity or user preference, leaves one scientific intervention.

## P-TRO-CONTACT-CONDITIONED-MEAN-DEVIATION-IMPEDANCE · version 1

**Principle.** Jointly rank nominal action and minimal normal-tangential impedance through future contact-conditioned mean and closed-loop deviation outcomes, and apply task, contact, actuator, gain-energy and deadline authority to that same published action.

**Mechanism.** Use cylinder-probe contact in T-RO; retain torque as the nominal action; optimize two contact-frame stiffness variables through a gain-conditioned deviation covariance rollout; derive damping from local effective mass and environment slopes; project the same sequence through contact, actuator, rate and tank constraints; publish verified SCAN or WITHDRAW. This changes T-RO's contact prediction formerly selected only feedforward torque while feedback impedance was predefined. R15 adds the feedback-controlled deviation state to the same prediction, so future contact determines both mean torque and closed-loop compliance without a second controller.. Probe contact distinctions affect the selected gain only through explicit A_cl, covariance or hard-margin paths. Torque and gains have independent authority, and the optimized member is also the energy-, constraint- and deadline-authorized applied member.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** Future deformable-contact prediction becomes a closed-loop compliance decision, not only a torque trajectory: one T-RO ADMM problem jointly chooses nominal torque and minimal normal-tangential impedance through separate mean and deviation outcome channels.

**Primary risks.** The minimal cylinder-probe contact and bounded local slopes contain the action-relevant scan envelope. → Escalate only the falsified contact term; persistent noncontainment kills the physical formulation.; Short-horizon local mean-deviation propagation preserves robust gain decisions. → Loss of action-order or hard-decision fidelity kills the reduced real-time method.; Two stiffness variables with state-derived damping are decision sufficient. → Promote damping or richer impedance only if a matched constrained rival dominates.; The tank conservatively accounts for positive energy injected by gain changes. → Freeze or decrease gains; the time-varying feedback-passivity claim fails.; A verified incumbent or preverified withdrawal can be published by every deadline. → The real-time scanning claim fails.

**Substantive difference.** Relative to T-RO, impedance is online and future-contact-conditioned. Relative to MPVIC, the plant is probe-specific deformable normal-tangential contact and the gains enter T-RO's DDP/IK/Projection relation. Relative to NNBO, impedance is the constrained action rather than an underdetermined realization of optimal wrench.
