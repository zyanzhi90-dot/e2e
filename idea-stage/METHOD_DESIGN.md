# Method Design — Candidate Principles

Design cycle: `MDCYCLE-SOFTSCAN-V2-R14-FULL-ENVELOPE-AUTHORITY`

This packet presents Candidate Principles for Human discussion and selection. It does not contain a test plan, execution set, or cost approval.

**Solution-space constraint.** UNDERCONSTRAINED: Both accepted RMCs require a history-bearing action-conditional outcome set and one deadline-total constrained impedance relation. Current formal Evidence does not independently eliminate a bounded hereditary finite-area physical family (P), a structurally constrained learned causal outcome family (L), or a physical skeleton plus bounded decision residual (H). All three are therefore complete executable Candidates; P is the strongest-simple priority, not an evidence-proven unique solution.

## P-HEREDITARY-CONTACT-CAPACITY-TOTAL-IMPEDANCE · version 4

**Principle.** A bounded hereditary finite-area normal-tangential contact-capacity set, governed by explicit calibration authority, is updated from scan history and consumed by one robust, energy-aware, deadline-total four-gain impedance relation.

**Mechanism.** Represent contact by nonnegative generalized-Maxwell normal memory and a bounded elasto-plastic tangential bristle/finite-area limit-surface state; maintain a drift-propagated fixed-facet parameter polytope from force/torque and kinematic consistency; propagate candidate positive normal/tangential stiffness-damping sequences through that set; and atomically apply only a completely verified robust scan member or the preverified unloading member at the update deadline. This changes NNBO's trial-fixed scalar point-contact law and proposal-then-realization chain become a history-bearing set-valued contact-capacity relation whose same member defines the optimized, energy-authorized, actuator-feasible and applied impedance action.. Normal relaxation, area evolution and tangential pre-slip/moment capacity are preserved exactly where they alter robust gain feasibility or ranking. Drift/reset set membership changes the predictive relation after an admitted calibration-out contact without treating confidence as authority. Robust multistep selection and a registered withdrawal make physical, energy, actuator and compute outcomes part of the applied-action relation rather than downstream corrections.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** A set-valued hereditary finite-area contact-capacity model defined by downstream four-gain decisions and embedded in one energy- and deadline-total applied-action relation; not a generic richer contact model and not a neural replacement of HC.

**Primary risks.** The declared envelope is contained by the hereditary finite-area physical family. → Persistent noncontainment or action-changing residual kills P.; Robot-side histories recover a useful containing contraction. → P cannot recover useful scanning after change.; Four gains are decision sufficient. → Use richer impedance.; Energy state conservatively contains exchange. → Hard energy authority fails.; Registered withdrawal is feasible under full envelope and WCET. → Action relation is not total.

**Substantive difference.** Relative to NNBO it changes scalar trial-fixed HC into online hereditary finite-area normal-tangential capacity and changes unconstrained one-axis optimal impedance into robust four-gain total action. Relative to deformable-contact MPC it selects impedance and adapts properties in contact. Relative to learned and adaptive MPVIC it makes bounded history/capacity uncertainty, not a point or mean model, authorize the applied scan action.

**Killer-test concepts.** held-out containment, action order, joint outcome and burden: Pattern A=P retains coverage with no persistent action-changing residual and is no worse at lower burden vs PRINCIPLE P-STRUCTURED-LEARNED-DECISION-OUTCOME-TOTAL-ACTION Pattern B=L/H preserves coverage while materially improving action/outcome or P is noncontaining under Declared continuous soft-contact scanning envelope; containment, contraction/action recovery and WCET after abrupt in-envelope change: Pattern A=chosen bounded update recovers a useful containing set without stale-margin relaxation vs PRINCIPLE P-BOUNDED-SET-MHE-ADAPTATION-RIVAL Pattern B=A bounded set-MHE or multiple-model update operating under the same full-envelope hard-authority invariant achieves equal-or-better containment, action recovery and WCET. under Abrupt and gradual admitted in-envelope changes, evaluated from the pre-detection cycle through recontraction; robust action order and joint outcome: Pattern A=four gains preserve action order/outcome within tolerance vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-OBJECTIVE-OR-TUNING-MISMATCH Pattern B=richer coupled impedance changes action or outcome under Declared continuous soft-contact scanning envelope; authorized action and measured energy balance: Pattern A=optimizer-internal energy is conservative and no post-hoc gain change occurs vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-POSTHOC-GUARD-EQUIVALENT Pattern B=post-hoc passivity is equally guaranteeing and action-equivalent under Declared continuous soft-contact scanning envelope; action definedness and constraints under forced timeout/infeasibility: Pattern A=every cycle publishes verified SCAN or WITHDRAW vs RIVAL_RCA ALT-COMPUTE-LATENCY-ONLY Pattern B=hold-last/incumbent is equally total without stale authority under Declared continuous soft-contact scanning envelope

## P-BOUNDED-SET-MHE-ADAPTATION-RIVAL · version 1

**Principle.** A bounded-horizon set-membership moving-horizon estimator maintains the candidate-specific authorized uncertainty object, while the same full-envelope-invariant robust four-gain SCAN/WITHDRAW relation determines action.

**Mechanism.** Replace the fixed-facet one-step intersection/update with bounded set-MHE over a finite observation window, jointly estimating contact state and parameters while preserving the identical independently calibrated full-envelope hard-authority invariant. This changes Only the online contraction estimator changes; representation, hard full-envelope constraints, robust gain action, energy state and total publication remain identical.. Set-MHE is a credible bounded-adaptation rival because windowed state/parameter consistency can improve contraction under latent contact memory, but it does not repair an additional accepted RMC beyond the simpler fixed-facet update.

**Provisional Scientific Delta.** None retained: this formal rival isolates estimator adaptation for the killer comparison.

**Primary risks.** Windowed joint estimation produces materially better containing contraction or action recovery than fixed-facet membership. → Without this benefit, extra state, tuning and WCET burden make set-MHE unnecessary.; The bounded-horizon estimator has certified WCET compatible with the control deadline. → It cannot replace the selected update.; Set-MHE never relaxes the independent full-envelope hard constraints. → It is inadmissible as a rival.

**Substantive difference.** Estimator window/state coupling only; all scientific representation and action mechanisms are held fixed.

**Killer-test concepts.** containment, contraction sharpness, recovered action order and WCET on identical abrupt/gradual changes: Pattern A=Set-MHE materially improves contraction or action recovery while retaining containment and WCET vs PRINCIPLE P-HEREDITARY-CONTACT-CAPACITY-TOTAL-IMPEDANCE Pattern B=Fixed-facet recursive membership is equal in containment/action recovery at lower burden under Identical admitted in-envelope change trajectories from pre-detection through recontraction

**Status.** REJECTED: REJECTED as a core Candidate by strongest-simple-rival necessity: no current Evidence establishes action-relevant benefit over recursive fixed-facet membership at equal authority and WCET. It remains the formally bound adaptation rival.
