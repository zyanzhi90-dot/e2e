- **Research question**: How can a robot select time-varying normal–tangential impedance online during continuous soft-contact scanning to minimize a joint force-regulation, trajectory-tracking, and contact-loss or slip objective while respecting wrench and interaction-energy constraints across material, geometry, sliding-condition, and contact-history shifts absent from its calibration data?
- **Observed phenomenon**: Identified-model optimal impedance, continuous finite-area soft-contact MPC, online constrained gain planning, model-agnostic six-dimensional compliant control, and uncertainty-aware force MPC each work in bounded regimes, but continuous scanning changes material response, local geometry, tangential resistance, and contact history, so the gain choice that best trades force, motion, and contact safety need not remain fixed or transferable beyond calibration.
- **Evidence-backed phenomenon**: Bound decision-grade evidence establishes every constituent capability and boundary, yet no accepted prior closes them into online coupled normal–tangential impedance selection across held-out soft-contact shifts with joint performance, wrench, interaction-energy, real-time, and failure-inclusive evaluation.
- **Evidence status**: The existing search is aligned rather than merely adjacent: it includes direct integrated searches for continuous robotic-ultrasound sliding soft contact and optimal impedance, plus targeted searches on viscoelastic state, tangential contact, passivity, uncertainty, real-time control, and independent scanning quality. Quality is CERTIFIED and novelty is NOVEL; empirical benefit remains unresolved.
- **Decisive evidence tier**: Decision-grade canonical Evidence Cards and Controller-bound Quality/Novelty verdicts establish the problem; later causal support requires matched held-out closed-loop comparisons, not contact-model fit alone.
- **Measurement validity**: Synchronize independently calibrated six-axis wrench, pose or indentation, commanded and rendered normal–tangential stiffness and damping, speed, solve timing, constraint activity, contact-loss or slip, and an independent scanning-quality endpoint; separate development from held-out specimens and conditions; randomize repeats; verify bandwidth, delay, drift, saturation, rendering fidelity, observability, and failure handling.
- **Artifact/confound alternatives**: Unequal data, sensing, compute, model, tuning, objective, horizon, inner-loop, or safety budgets; specimen or condition leakage; impedance-rendering error; force/torque drift, filtering, delay, saturation, gravity compensation, and coupling; geometry or contact-area error; medium, lubrication, temperature, hydration, friction, adhesion, or aging shifts; solver infeasibility, timeout, warm-start, actuator saturation, and fallback behavior; and task-quality perception shift.

- **Prevalence/effect scale**: The control conflict recurs across optimal impedance interaction, robotic-ultrasound scanning, deformable-contact MPC, six-dimensional compliant control, soft-tissue force control, and uncertainty-aware MPC. Component methods report meaningful nominal gains, but the magnitude and robustness of online optimal-impedance benefit under held-out continuous soft-contact shifts remain unmeasured.
- **Scope and boundary**: Rigid ultrasound-like probes scanning compliant finite-area surfaces; bounded normal and tangential stiffness and damping updated more slowly than the inner loop; held-out material, relaxation, curvature, thickness, speed, direction, dwell, history, tangential resistance, and disturbances; excludes universal soft-body modeling, unrestricted exploration, clinical efficacy, universal injury limits, and any precommitment to a contact model, learning architecture, optimizer, critic, or passivity device.
- **Why it matters**: Impedance is the robot's direct mechanical policy for trading tracking precision against compliant and safe interaction. If it cannot be selected reliably as contact conditions change, stronger models, estimators, or learning modules do not by themselves produce better control.
- **Value if yes / value if no**: A positive result establishes a transferable online impedance-selection principle and the smallest information set needed for held-out gains within hard constraints; a negative result establishes decision-equivalence of robust fixed, scheduled, model-agnostic, or predefined-impedance predictive control and prevents unnecessary sensing, identification, learning, and optimization complexity.
- **Decision owner / threshold**: The independent Problem Quality and Problem Novelty Gates followed by the human Problem Gate own problem acceptance. Later empirical support requires improvement beyond predeclared sensor- and task-relevant margins in joint force–motion–contact cost or regret on held-out conditions, satisfaction of every wrench, contact, interaction-energy, actuator, and compute limit, and superiority to the strongest matched comparators without material loss on any primary endpoint; timeouts and fallbacks count against the selector.
- **Plausible explanations**: Optimal gains change because material, geometry, friction, and history alter the contact-dependent closed-loop map; strong measured-wrench or predictive feedback may absorb most variation; apparent benefit may be resource or tuning bias; partial observability may prevent timely discrimination; or hard wrench, energy, computation, and rendering constraints may dominate feasible gain choice.
- **Decisive probe or falsifier**: The claim is falsified if the strongest matched conventional baseline is equivalent or better on the joint objective and every primary endpoint across the held-out envelope, if apparent benefit vanishes after resource equalization, or if no online selector improves performance without violating wrench, energy, contact, actuator, or real-time limits.
- **Feasible discriminating probe**: Use a randomized repeated-measures benchtop scanning benchmark over held-out soft phantoms, curvatures or thicknesses, speeds and directions, dwell and loading histories, tangential resistance changes, and bounded disturbances; establish a condition-informed offline reference; compare robust fixed impedance, scheduling, model-agnostic six-dimensional admittance, online constrained impedance planning, reproduced identified-contact optimal impedance, and contact-aware predefined-impedance MPC under matched resources.

None is causally equivalent to coupled online normal–tangential impedance selection across uncalibrated material, geometry, sliding, and history shifts with joint failure-inclusive wrench, energy, and real-time evaluation.
